Founded in the Vallée de Joux — the cradle of haute horlogerie — Audemars Piguet has defied convention for over 150 years. Every timepiece a manifesto. Every movement a revolution in mechanical artistry.

The Royal Oak, born in 1972 from the genius of Gérald Genta, shattered the notion that luxury watches must be round and precious. Its octagonal bezel and integrated bracelet became the most recognizable silhouette in watchmaking.

ROYAL OAK

DOUBLE BALANCE WHEEL OPENWORKED

A 41mm masterpiece in 18-karat pink gold. The openworked Calibre 3132 reveals 255 hand-finished components through an anti-reflective sapphire crystal. Two balance wheels and two hairsprings deliver unparalleled precision — a mechanical triumph visible through the skeletonized architecture.

AUDEMARS PIGUET
ROYAL OAK

SELFWINDING 36MM

The Royal Oak Selfwinding 36mm in stainless steel — an icon refined to its purest expression. Ref. 26674ST.OO.1320ST.01 houses Calibre 5800 within the legendary octagonal bezel, delivering 50 hours of power reserve. Integrated steel bracelet, Grande Tapisserie dial, every detail an act of precision.

AUDEMARS PIGUET
ROYAL OAK

SELFWINDING MINI

The Royal Oak Mini — where architectural heritage meets feminine elegance. A 34mm statement in stainless steel with the unmistakable octagonal bezel, Grande Tapisserie dial, and supple integrated bracelet. Precision scaled to perfection, novelty distilled to its essence.
